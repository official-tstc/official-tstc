# The Software Technology Company

[Click to visit the website](https://official-tstc.github.io/web)

TSTC is a company focused on developing affordable products, whether free or with a one-time payment, without subscriptions, without intentionally worsening the user experience to force them to pay. No selling or abusive use of customer data takes place here.

TSTC is also responsible for publishing and developing games, using creativity to bring art to the world. With a unique artistic vision, contributing to the indie gaming community

Founded on August 10, 2024, in Brazil by [George Júnior](https://github.com/georgejrdev).
